# HEARTS — a tale of Yelärk

An 8-bit adventure for Laritza. Player 1 is pulled across the dimensions to Yelärk, a city on the planet Frusto that mirrors Earth. Guided by a mysterious Voice, she must gather 16 hearts to find her way home.

**Stages:** Arrival Hall (tutorial) · IAB Top Floor · East Asian Library · The Brain Lab · Levien Gym · Notre Dame Parish · Boss: Grimble · The 7 Train · Queens at Night · Snack Storm · Poppa Jac's Peaches · Boss: Mothrael · The Stargazing Site · The Peach Phone · Labyrinth of Miles · Boss: Malvoren · October 26

**Controls:** Arrows / WASD to move · Z / Space to act · Enter to start · M toggles music · R resets the block puzzle.
On phones: on-screen D-pad and A button (best played on a laptop).

All story text lives in the `CONFIG` block at the top of `index.html`.
