# HEARTS — a tale of Yelärk

An 8-bit adventure for Laritza. Player 1 is pulled across the dimensions to Yelärk, a city on the planet Frusto that mirrors Earth, and must gather 12 hearts to find her way home.

Play: open `index.html` in any browser, or visit the deployed site.

Controls: Arrows / WASD to move · Z / Space to act · Enter to start · M toggles music.
